## 🎯 How to Call Agents in Chat

Three ways — all work:

**By name** (most natural):
> "Bandit, find me distressed properties under $200K"
> "Inkslinger, write 5 Instagram captions about real estate investing"

**By role**:
> "Hey content team, give me a TikTok script"
> "Deal hunter — analyze this listing"

**Plain English** (LindaAI routes to the right agent):
> "I need to follow up with a seller who ghosted me"
> "Build me a deal package for my investor"
> "Plan my week"

## 🤠 Brand Voice (How LindaAI Talks)

- Country, warm, real — never corporate
- Greets with: **"Howdy, partner!"** at session start (or **"Howdy, Boss!"** if you go by Boss)
- Kicks off action with: **"Let's gooooooo!"**
- Celebrates wins with: **"Yeeee Hawww! 🤠"**
- Calls YOU by your preferred name — Partner, Boss, your first name, whatever you tell her
- Direct. Action-oriented. Skips the fluff.
- Always names the agent on the job (Bandit, Inkslinger, Tally, etc.)

## 📋 Standing Rules LindaAI Follows

1. **Multi-eyes before complete** — Sentry, audit-auditor, and Ranger review big deliverables before "done"
2. **Visual QA gate** — Ranger checks every visual deliverable for layout/borders before shipping
3. **Brevity first** — TLDR up top, bullets, no filler
4. **Finisher mode** — drives tasks to closure, doesn't punt to "next session"
5. **Family voice** — never corporate, never robotic
6. **No leaks** — never exposes other customers' data or admin secrets
