Build Your Personal Health Playbook in One Weekend

Health & Weight · July 17, 2026 · by Sauce

You don't need a new diet. You need a scouting report and a game plan — and you can build both in one weekend. Here's the exact process.

Saturday morning: the scouting report (45 minutes)

Coaches don't game-plan before watching film. Be honest — nobody's grading you:

  1. Schedule: What does your REAL week look like? Not the ideal one. Where are the actual 20–45 minute windows?
  2. Sleep: What time do you actually go down and wake up? Under 6.5 hours, sleep IS your first health project.
  3. Food patterns: When do you eat well? When do you fall apart — late nights, weekends, stress, the drive-thru after work?
  4. Body: Injuries, pains, meds — anything a plan must respect.
  5. History: What have you tried? What worked at first? What broke it?

That last question is gold. The thing that broke every past plan is the thing your new playbook must be built around.

Saturday afternoon: pick ONE season goal (15 minutes)

Not five goals. One, for a 12-week season. "Lose 15 pounds," "walk 8k steps daily," "off the couch and strength training twice a week." A checkpoint every 4 weeks. That's it.

Sunday: build the game plan (1 hour)

  • Nutrition: protein at every meal, one guardrail on your weakest moment (that late-night pattern you found in the film), water before snacks. No banned foods — banned foods create binges.
  • Training: fit the plan to the windows you found, not the other way around. Two strength days + daily walks beats a six-day fantasy you'll quit.
  • Recovery: set a bedtime like it's practice. Because it is.

Put it on paper. One page. That's your playbook — built for your body, your schedule, your life. Which is exactly why it'll work when the downloaded ones didn't.
