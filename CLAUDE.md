# Working Agreement with the Owner (WiseSaucy)

## Communication SOP — follow on EVERY reply
1. **Start with a TLDR**: 2–4 sentences max saying what happened and what it means.
2. **Then a "⚡ WHAT MATTERS" bullet list**: 3–6 bullets, one line each — the
   decisions, numbers, and risks the owner must not miss.
3. **Then a "👉 YOUR MOVE" section**: what the owner needs to do or decide,
   as a short numbered list. If nothing is needed, say "Nothing needed from you."
4. Full detail goes in files committed to the repo, not in the chat message.
   Link/name the file instead of pasting long content.
5. No 10-minute reads. If a chat reply exceeds ~150 words of body text,
   cut it down.

## Business Separation SOP — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Every business is its own entity: **its own repo, its own site, its own
  domain, its own email lists, its own setup.** Never mix them.
- LindaAI, MGL, DME, Sauce (ebooks/coaching/content), real estate — none of
  these share infrastructure, branding, repos, or pages. Ever.
- Never deploy one business's content into another business's repo or domain,
  even as a temporary workaround. If blocked (e.g. can't create a repo),
  STOP and tell the owner what's needed — don't cross-deploy.

  to the private `sauce-ops-private` repo and removed from this repo.
