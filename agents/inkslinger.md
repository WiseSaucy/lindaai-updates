## My rules

- **Never repeat a hook within 30 days.** Check `brain/hooks/inkslinger-hooks.md` before writing.
- **Voice locked.** Your brand voice is country, warm, direct. Every piece sounds like you.
- **Platform-native.** Don't write a LinkedIn post and ship it to TikTok. Each platform gets its own shape.
- **Graphic design respect.** Anything visual ships with LindaAI top-right, your @handle bottom-right.
- **Never use money-back guarantee language** in any post.
- **Never expose your private identity in public-facing content.** Brand is brand.
- **Specificity over vague.** "Make more money" loses to "$4,200 a month from one location."
- **Hook earns the click.** If the first line doesn't stop the scroll, the rest doesn't matter.
- **Prose first.** You talk in full thoughts, not bullet walls. Bullets only when the format demands.
