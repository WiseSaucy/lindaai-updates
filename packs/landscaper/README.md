# 🌿 Landscaper Pack

**Routes, estimates, crews, and seasonal services — your green-industry command center.**

Howdy, partner! If you're out there mowing, mulching, building hardscapes, blowing leaves, or plowing snow — your business runs on routes, crews, and follow-through. This pack handles the office side so you can stay in the field. Let's gooooooo!

---

## What's in this pack

Fourteen core skills built for the landscaping and lawn care grind:

- **linda-route** — Optimize your daily routes — crew, drive time, fuel, sequencing.
- **linda-estimate** — Build clean, professional estimates fast — labor, materials, markup, optional add-ons.
- **linda-crew** — Crew scheduling and assignments — who's on what truck, what jobs, what hours.
- **linda-seasonal** — Spring cleanup, fall blowouts, snow contracts, holiday lights — seasonal service planning.
- **linda-propcard** — Property card per customer — gate codes, dog notes, pet allergies, special instructions.
- **linda-visitlog** — Log every visit — what got done, what didn't, photos, time on site.
- **linda-yardposts** — Per-platform lawn-care content (TikTok before/afters, IG transformations, FB neighborhood specials, YouTube how-tos) — seasonal CTAs, hands off straight to `/linda-post-walkthrough`.
- **linda-yardleads** — Lead funnel — capture from web/phone/door-knock, score by lot size + route density + service type, auto-draft quotes, drive follow-up cadence.
- **linda-invoice** — Generate invoices on the spot — recurring, one-time, contract billing.
- **linda-followup** — Track every promise — "I'll send a quote Friday" never falls through again.
- **linda-pulse** — Quick status across every property and contract — who's behind, who needs attention.
- **linda-leads** — Lead pipeline — track every prospect from "called in" to "signed contract."
- **linda-mail** — Draft pro emails — quote follow-ups, customer updates, contract renewals.
- **linda-kpi** — KPIs that matter — revenue per crew hour, retention, average ticket, route density.

You also get four agents on call: **Wrangler** (BD/partnerships), **Holler** (social media), **Tally** (data), and **Ranger** (your virtual assistant).

Lawn care companies, full-service landscapers, hardscape installers, snow removal operators, and tree services running 1–10 crews. Whether you're a solo operator with a trailer and a mower or you've got 6 trucks rolling daily, this pack keeps the office tight while you keep the customers happy.

Made for the folks who'd rather be on a Z-turn than in front of a laptop.
