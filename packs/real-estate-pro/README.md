## What's in this pack

**Eighteen skills**, organized by where they fit in the deal lifecycle. Hand off cleanly between them — `/linda-bandit` finds the deal, `/linda-deals` underwrites it, `/linda-loi` fires the offer, `/linda-closer` pushes it across the line, `/linda-zillow` + `/linda-retiktok` market it. Every skill is built around one of LindaAI's named agents (Bandit, Closer, Wrangler, Inkslinger) so you always know who's on the job.

### 🤠 Hunt the deal
- **linda-bandit** — 🤠 **Bandit** hunts MHP, RV, wholesale, and off-market deals matching your buy box. Ranks by motivation + math. Tells you which 3 to call first.
- **linda-property** — Deep-dive any address. Owner, tax records, sales history, neighborhood data, estimated value.
- **linda-rents** — Pull rent comps, vacancy data, and tell you what to charge — long-term, Section 8, MTR, or short-term.

### 📊 Underwrite the deal
- **linda-deals** — Underwrite any property in seconds. Cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, DSCR, multi-scenario exit math.
- **linda-invest** — Run BRRRR, flip, buy-and-hold, seller-finance amortization, and creative finance scenarios side-by-side.
- **linda-airbnb** — Underwrite a short-term rental with real Airbnb data and seasonality.
- **linda-coliving** — Rent-by-the-room analysis for co-living, sober living, and house hacks.
- **linda-rvpark** — Underwrite any RV park or campground. 5 key metrics, seller numbers vs. three offers (conventional / partial seller carry / full seller carry), NOI normalization, screening hacks, and the bad-day stress test.

### 🚂 Make the offer
- **linda-loi** — Send a clean, professional LOI in minutes — DSCR senior + seller carry junior structure built in, signable PDF, auto-follow-ups.
- **linda-outreach** — Draft seller letters, texts, voicemails, and cold call scripts that actually get replies.
- **linda-voicedeal** — Talk a contract into existence — voice memo your terms, get a signed-ready agreement.

### 💼 Close the deal
- **linda-closer** — 💼 **Closer** is your sales boss for after the LOI. Objection handling, follow-up cadence, pipeline review, closing scripts.
- **linda-negotiate** — Build a full negotiation strategy — BATNA, leverage, objection scripts, conversation flow.
- **linda-pipeline** — See every deal in your pipeline. Velocity, stale deals, what's closing this week.

### 🤝 Build the network
- **linda-wrangler** — 🤝 **Wrangler** raises capital, builds JV partnerships, sources brokers/lenders/GCs/PMs, and structures equity splits + waterfalls.

### ✍️ Market the deal
- **linda-dealpack** — Spin up a marketing package — one-pager, social posts, listing copy, investor pitch — for any deal.
- **linda-zillow** — ✍️ **Inkslinger** writes listing-ready content for Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, MLS, FSBO sites, plus social cross-post for TikTok/IG/FB/YT/X. Includes headlines, description, highlights, FAQ.
- **linda-retiktok** — ✍️ **Inkslinger** batches 60-second TikTok / Reels / Shorts scripts for MHP, RV, wholesale, BRRRR, and creative finance content. Hook → Value → CTA format. Outputs `PUBLISH_PACK.md` for `/linda-post-walkthrough`.
