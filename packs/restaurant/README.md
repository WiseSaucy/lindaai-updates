# 🍽️ Restaurant Owner Pack

**Inventory, shifts, reviews, menu engineering — front-of-house and back-of-house, handled.**

Howdy, partner! Running a restaurant is one of the toughest gigs there is — tight margins, long hours, picky guests, and a thousand decisions a day. This pack keeps the business side sharp so you can focus on the food. Let's gooooooo!

---

## What's in this pack

Twelve core skills built for restaurant owners, GMs, and food-service operators — every one of them fully built and ready to run today:

**Operations (BOH/FOH):**
- **linda-inventory** — Track inventory, par levels, waste, and food cost percentage; auto-builds reorder sheets for Sysco/US Foods/PFG.
- **linda-shifts** — Build the weekly staff schedule fast — labor cost forecasting, time-off requests, coverage gaps flagged.
- **linda-menu** — Menu engineering — categorize every item into Stars / Plowhorses / Puzzles / Dogs with margin-boosting actions.

**Customer & reputation:**
- **linda-reviews** — Draft warm, brand-on replies to Google / Yelp / TripAdvisor reviews — 5-star love and 1-star takes alike.
- **linda-followup** — Post-visit guest thanks, catering quote nudges, vendor callbacks, win-back sequences — drafted in seconds.
- **linda-mail** — Audience-aware email drafter — vendor (firm), staff (warm), customer (gracious), landlord/regulator (paper-trail).

**Sales & events:**
- **linda-leads** — Lead pipeline for catering, private events, and corporate accounts — auto-scored 0-100, HOT leads routed to followup.
- **linda-invoice** — Branded restaurant invoices — deposit, balance, full — for catering, private events, corporate accounts.

**Numbers:**
- **linda-pulse** — Weekly restaurant heartbeat — covers, sales, labor %, food cost, top issues vs targets — under 60 seconds.
- **linda-kpi** — Deep KPI dashboard — prime cost, table turn, RevPASH, comp YoY — every leak called out in dollars.

**Marketing & content:**
- **linda-posts** — Per-platform social content (TikTok / IG / FB / YT Shorts / Twitter) for specials, BTS, events — outputs a `PUBLISH_PACK.md` ready for `/linda-post-walkthrough`.
- **linda-calendar** — Daily/weekly/monthly content calendar mapping specials, events, and holidays to posts — with batched filming plans.

You also get a team of LindaAI agents on call. The restaurant pack leans hardest on **🩺 Doc** (customer support), **🐎 Pony** (email + follow-up), **📣 Holler** (social), **📊 Tally** (numbers/KPIs), **🤝 Wrangler** (BD/catering leads), **💼 Closer** (billing/sales), and **🪖 Ranger** (general right-hand) — all named, all on the job.

Built for owners who want to stop drowning in spreadsheets and start running a tighter, more profitable house.
