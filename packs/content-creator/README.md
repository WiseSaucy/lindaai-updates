## What's in this pack

Twelve core skills built for full-time and part-time creators across short-form video, long-form YouTube, and written platforms:

- **linda-hooks** — Generate 20+ scroll-stopping hooks for any topic, filterable by platform.
- **linda-script** — Short-form video script writer — hook/setup/payoff/CTA in 30s, 60s, and 90s cuts.
- **linda-batch** — Plan and write 7-30 days of content in one run, mapped to platforms and dates.
- **linda-branddeal** — Brand deal pipeline — pitches, rates, deliverables, contracts, payments.
- **linda-analytics** — Cross-platform analytics rollup — TikTok, IG, YouTube — weekly trend report.
- **linda-thumbnail** — YouTube thumbnail concept generator — title + 5 concepts + designer spec sheet.
- **linda-posts** — Generate social posts on demand — captions, hashtags, hooks for any platform.
- **linda-remix** — Turn one piece of content into 10+ derivatives across every platform.
- **linda-calendar** — Plan a content calendar for 1-4 weeks — posts mapped to platforms and dates.
- **linda-mail** — Draft pro emails — sponsor outreach, manager comms, fan replies, press.
- **linda-pulse** — Daily creator pulse — what's posting today, what's filming, what's overdue.
- **linda-kpi** — KPIs that matter for creators — followers, views, watch time, CTR, RPM, deal $.

You also get three agents on call: **Inkslinger** (content machine), **Holler** (social media manager), and **Drawl** (copywriter).
