# 🧭 Life Coach Pack

**Client intake, session notes, programs, accountability — your coaching practice on autopilot.**

Howdy, partner! Coaching is one part transformation work, two parts staying organized — and most coaches drown in the second part. This pack handles intake, session notes, program design, accountability, the master client tracker, and the content engine, so you can spend your hours on the calls that change lives. Let's gooooooo!

---

## What's in this pack

Fourteen core skills tuned for life, business, fitness, and mindset coaches:

- **linda-intake** — Run a clean new-client intake — goals, history, accountability prefs, payment plan, branded PDF + welcome email.
- **linda-session** — Log per-session notes — wins, blocks, action items, homework, next session, risk flags.
- **linda-program** — Design N-week coaching programs — weekly themes, exercises, milestones, deliverables list, full PDF curriculum.
- **linda-accountability** — Run the check-in cadence — daily/weekly messages, completion tracking, missed-week alerts.
- **linda-clientcoach** — Master client tracker — program, sessions, payment, breakthroughs, churn-risk score, roster view.
- **linda-coachcontent** — Coaching content engine — testimonial posts (permission-gated), mini-lessons, weekly newsletter.
- **linda-coachposts** — Per-platform coaching content (TikTok hooks, IG carousels, FB Reels, YouTube Shorts, X/Twitter) — bilingual EN/ES, discovery-call CTAs, hands off straight to `/linda-post-walkthrough`.
- **linda-coachleads** — Discovery-call funnel — capture leads, score fit/heat/reach, schedule, pre-call brief, post-call routing, nurture sequence.
- **linda-invoice** — Generate clean invoices — single sessions, packages, monthly retainers, program payment plans.
- **linda-followup** — Track every coach commitment — recap promised, intro promised, resource promised.
- **linda-pulse** — Daily pulse on every active client — who's progressing, who's stalled, who needs attention now.
- **linda-leads** — Lead pipeline from discovery call inquiry to signed client — stages, follow-ups, conversion stats.
- **linda-mail** — Draft client communications — recaps, re-engagement, alumni nurture, program announcements.
- **linda-calendar** — Plan content + program rollouts — weekly themes, launch sequences, evergreen rotations.

You also get five agents on call: **Grit** (personal development specialist), **Doc** (customer support), **Holler** (social media), **Wrangler** (business development), and **Ranger** (virtual assistant / right-hand).

## Coaching ≠ Therapy

LindaAI is built to support coaching practices, not provide clinical care. Coaching is not therapy or medical advice. If a client mentions a mental health crisis, suicidal ideation, abuse, or a medical issue, refer them to a licensed professional immediately. Every client-facing skill in this pack carries this disclaimer for a reason — protect your clients and protect your practice.
