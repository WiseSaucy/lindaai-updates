# 🔧 HVAC / Plumbing Pack

**Dispatch, service tickets, maintenance contracts — the trades shop that runs itself.**

Howdy, partner! Whether you're turning wrenches on furnaces, water heaters, A/C units, or sewer lines, your business lives and dies by the dispatch board. This pack hooks up your office to your trucks like nothing else. Let's gooooooo!

---

## What's in this pack

Fifteen core skills built for HVAC, plumbing, and home-services trades:

- **linda-dispatch** — Daily dispatch board — assign techs, route calls, manage emergencies.
- **linda-emergency** — 24/7 emergency-call intake — safety triage, urgency tiering, on-call paging, post-job callback.
- **linda-ticket** — Service ticket creation and tracking — diagnosis, parts, labor, customer sign-off.
- **linda-maintain** — Maintenance contract management — annual tune-ups, renewals, recurring revenue.
- **linda-parts** — Parts inventory and order tracking — never get caught short on a job.
- **linda-warranty** — Warranty tracking — what's covered, what's expired, claim documentation.
- **linda-techroute** — Optimize tech routes — drive time, call density, skill matching.
- **linda-gmb** — Google Business Profile engine — review replies (good and bad), weekly GBP posts, photo prompts to the crew, Q&A drafter. Hands off to `/linda-post-walkthrough`.
- **linda-trades-leads** — Lead funnel with tier scoring (Hot/Warm/Cold) by ticket potential + urgency + source — auto-routes to dispatch, in-person estimate, or nurture.
- **linda-invoice** — Generate invoices on the spot — diagnostic, repair, install, contract billing.
- **linda-followup** — Track every callback — quotes, repairs needed, second-visit installs.
- **linda-pulse** — Quick pulse on every open ticket and active job — what's hot, what's stuck.
- **linda-leads** — Lead pipeline from web form to signed estimate to closed job.
- **linda-mail** — Draft pro emails — quote follow-ups, install schedule confirmations, contract renewals.
- **linda-kpi** — KPIs that matter — average ticket, callback rate, contract renewal rate, revenue per tech hour.

You also get five agents on call: **Wrangler** (BD/partnerships), **Doc** (customer support / emergency intake), **Holler** (social media / GBP), **Tally** (data), and **Ranger** (your virtual assistant).

HVAC contractors, plumbing companies, drain cleaning specialists, and home-services trades running 1–15 trucks. Whether it's just you and a van or a full team with a CSR up front, this pack moves your dispatch, ticketing, and follow-up game out of sticky notes and into a sharp, focused system.

Built for owner-operators who value uptime, callbacks, and keeping techs billable.
