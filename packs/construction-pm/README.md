# 🏗️ Construction PM Pack

**Daily field reports, WIP tracking, and project health — without leaving the jobsite.**

Howdy, partner! Running a construction shop means you're juggling crews, change orders, submittals, invoices, and a phone that won't quit. This pack is built to take that load off your shoulders. Let's gooooooo!

---

## What's in this pack

Sixteen core skills tuned for general contractors, project managers, and construction operators:

- **linda-dwr** — Log a Daily Work Report in 30 seconds — crew, weather, hours, progress, photos.
- **linda-wip** — Work-In-Progress tracking. See exactly where every project stands financially.
- **linda-projecttrack** — Master schedule for every active job — milestones, deadlines, dependencies.
- **linda-jobhealth** — Project health score. Red/yellow/green on schedule, budget, and crew load.
- **linda-changeorder** — Draft and track change orders — clean documentation, clean billing.
- **linda-submittal** — Submittal log and tracking — what's out, what's approved, what's overdue.
- **linda-rfi** — Draft and track RFIs — keep the ball-in-court clear, flag cost/schedule impact before it becomes a surprise CO.
- **linda-schedule** — Build the schedule, find the critical path, and hand the field a clean 2–3 week look-ahead.
- **linda-subs** — Subcontractor directory, COI/insurance expiration tracking, and lien waivers — no waiver, no payment.
- **linda-punchlist** — Run the punch list to closeout — assign by trade, verify before close, release retainage clean.
- **linda-invoice** — Generate professional invoices — progress billing, retainage, line items.
- **linda-mail** — Draft any email — owner updates, sub coordination, RFIs, vendor notes.
- **linda-pulse** — Quick status pulse across every active project — what's hot, what's stuck.
- **linda-kpi** — Track the numbers that matter — gross margin, schedule slip, change order velocity.
- **sop-builder** — Build SOPs for every repeatable process — onboarding subs, closing out jobs, punch lists.
- **hiring-screener** — Hiring screener for PMs, supers, foremen, and field labor.

You also get three agents on call: **Tally** (data analyst), **Wrangler** (BD/sub coordination), and **Scout** (recruiter).

## Who this is for

General contractors, design-build firms, custom home builders, commercial PMs, and construction owners running 3–30 active projects. If you're tired of hunting through email threads to find the latest change order or trying to remember which sub said what last Tuesday, this pack is for you.

Built for folks who run their business off a clipboard and a truck — not a fancy enterprise dashboard.
